BigChange Value

Two-Way Fixflo Sync & Automated Gas Safety Certificates

Work orders from Fixflo drop automatically into BigChange's unscheduled jobs bucket with full tenant/agent details. Completed gas safety certificates and routine service reports sync straight back—giving your office hours back every week.

Current Bottleneck

Protecting Internal Part Prices & Complex Job Notes

Routine services and gas safeties can go straight to agents, but complex jobs or further-works notes containing internal part prices need office review before landlords see them.

"Gas safety services send, yeah fine—but for more complex jobs, I wouldn't want to send straight away."
BigChange Value

Outcome-Based Routing & Further-Works Hold Buckets

Jobs marked "Further Works / Parts Required" route into an office follow-up bucket rather than a green completed closure—while Matthew is confirming exact selective hold/push controls with BigChange's Fixflo specialist ahead of Wednesday's call.

Current Bottleneck

Preserving 4 Years of Private Client Job History

Switching systems raises concerns about losing 4 years of Commusoft historical job reports when private clients ask what was fitted last year.

"If a private client says 'you were here last year', it's so easy to refer to... that's a good idea, using it as read-only."
BigChange Value

Clean CSV Contact Migration + 1 Read-Only Archive Seat

Import all customer contacts, addresses, and sites cleanly into BigChange via CSV for a fresh start, while keeping just 1 Commusoft back-office seat as a low-cost read-only reference archive for 6–12+ months until BigChange holds your full history.

Current Bottleneck

Engineer Travel Accountability, ETAs & Van Checks

Limited visibility when new hires take too long between local patch jobs, manual appointment confirmations, and no formal 7-day van checks.

"We've had a new hire and he's getting a bit on my nerves with his time taking... there is no hiding."

Free Hardwired GPS, Live Chevrons & Automated ETAs

Complimentary hardwired vehicle trackers display live traffic chevrons and journey replays, send automated booking confirmations and "on route" ETAs to residents, and enforce simple 1-photo weekly van checks to protect your business legally.

Current Bottleneck

Separate Quoting, Invoicing & Xero Reconciliation

Private quotes, Invoice2go invoices, WhatsApp supplier receipts, and Xero live across separate tools—pulling Bianca into day-to-day admin instead of working on the business.

"I'm trying to take a bit more of a back step from the main office... having one system saving time anyway."

Brochure Quotes, Engineer Profit Reports & Two-Way Xero
